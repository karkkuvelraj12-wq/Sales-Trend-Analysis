## Context
This project uses a reproducible simulated dataset of 2,000 orders across four categories during 2025. The results demonstrate an analysis workflow and are not real company performance.

## Recommendations
1. Investigate what drove the peak month and test repeatable tactics in lower-revenue months.
2. Review the top category alongside gross margin, stock availability, and conversion before increasing investment.
3. Pilot a loyalty offer for high-spend customers and measure incremental margin and repeat purchases.

## Next steps
Validate these patterns with authorized real sales data. Add costs, discounts, refunds, and customer acquisition costs before making commercial decisions.
